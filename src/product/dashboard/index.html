About QASense

AI-assisted quality intelligence for modern software.

QASense AI is a multimodal AI-assisted software quality platform that combines defect-risk prediction, transformer-based source-code localization, computer vision, browser runtime evidence, and deterministic QA reasoning to produce explainable findings, test recommendations, and regression priorities.
